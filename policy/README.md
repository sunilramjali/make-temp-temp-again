# Policy

Documents and notes for methodology step 3 (support and move-on routes) and step 5 (spending evidence).

## Folders

- `national/`: the legal and policy framework that applies to every borough. For example:
  - Housing Act 1996 Part 7 (homelessness duties) and the Homelessness Reduction Act 2017
  - Homelessness Code of Guidance for Local Authorities
  - Homelessness (Suitability of Accommodation) (England) Order 2003 (the six-week limit on B&B for families with children and pregnant women)
  - Suitability of Accommodation Order 2012 (private rented sector offers, out-of-area placements)
- `newham/`: Newham's homelessness and rough sleeping strategy, allocations scheme, TA placement or procurement policy, private rented sector access and incentive schemes, cabinet and budget reports covering TA spending.
- `comparators/<borough>/`: the equivalent documents for each comparator borough.

## Conventions

- File name: `YYYY-MM_publisher_short-title.pdf`, e.g. `2024-03_newham_allocations-scheme.pdf`.
- Log every document in `services_map.csv` (services) or `data/sources.csv` (figures).
- Record **what is offered** separately from **evidence of uptake or outcomes**. A published scheme is not evidence that it works.
